01 / PROCUREMENT METHODOLOGY

A Clear Process from Start to Success

Our 5-stage procurement framework guarantees transparency, uncompromised quality, and punctuality at every milestone.

01

Understand Your Needs

We analyze your architectural drawings, bills of quantities (BOQ), technical tolerances, target budget, and required delivery timeline to determine the most competitive manufacturing route in China.

02

Source & Verify Factory Capacity

Our team stationed on the ground in Foshan and Guangzhou performs on-site audits of certified tier-1 factories. We examine production lines, verify ISO/CE/SNI credentials, and send physical sample boxes for your client approval.

03

Procurement & Quality Control (QC)

During manufacturing, our engineers conduct continuous inline checks. We perform shade variation tests, dimensional checks, stress tests, and pre-packing inspections, ensuring zero defective units enter the container.

04

Delivery & Port Clearance to Indonesia

Full international export packing with reinforced fumigated timber crates. We coordinate sea freight, bill of lading, Indonesian customs clearance, and inland delivery direct to your job site or warehouse.

05

After-Sales & Continuous Replenishment

We supervise container de-stuffing, provide installation technical advisory, manage defect warranty replacements, and establish continuous repeat supply channels for multi-phase projects.

02 / QUALITY CONTROL

The 8-Point Pre-Shipment Inspection

Before any container door is sealed in China, our QA engineers execute mandatory testing protocols.

01. DIMENSIONAL

Caliper Precision

Laser micrometer measurement of diagonal, thickness, and edge straightness to within ±0.2mm tolerance.

02. COLOR TONE

Shade Matching

Spectrophotometer analysis and dry-lay inspection under standard D65 daylight simulator booths.

03. POROSITY

Water Absorption

Vacuum boiling test confirming porcelain water absorption remains strictly under 0.1% for frost and mold proofing.

04. SURFACE

Hardness & Stain

Mohs hardness scratch testing and 24-hour methylene blue stain resistance evaluation.

03 / FULL TRANSPARENCY

Live Production Photo & Video Reporting

Never wonder what is happening to your order in China. We assign a dedicated bilingual project coordinator who streams high-definition video inspections, sends batch photo logs, and provides container loading count verification directly via WhatsApp.

Inspection Report Format: PDF Certificate + High-Res Photos
Container Sealing Verification: Customs Bolt Seal Serial Log
04 / CAPITAL PROTECTION

Risk Mitigation & Escrow Protection

Protecting your capital against supply chain fraud, factory insolvency, and material non-conformity.

Indonesian Legal Contracts

Contracts governed under Indonesian commercial law through our registered corporate entity in Surabaya & Jakarta.

Factory Escrow Holdback

Chinese manufacturers only receive final settlement after our engineering team clears container stuffing inspections.

Transit Cargo Insurance

Full Institute Cargo Clauses (A) all-risk maritime insurance covering 110% of total invoice value from warehouse to site.
