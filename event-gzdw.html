Guangzhou Design Week 2024

Home / Events / Guangzhou Design Week 2024
Guangzhou, China • December 6–9, 2024 • Official GZDW Strategic Partner

Vision Focus participated in Guangzhou Design Week 2024 as the official registered partner in Indonesia. During the Indonesia session, Mr. Jason Shi delivered the keynote address on economic growth, art and design trends, and end-to-end direct factory supply chain management connecting Indonesian projects with premier Chinese manufacturers.
